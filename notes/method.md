# Method: harness, loop, graph

People mix these words. They are different altitudes.

## Harness

Everything around the model: sandbox, filesystem memory, git as state, tools, permissions, traces. The room moves the score more than a model bump.

## Loop

Repeated act → verify → revise with a hard stop. Context resets. Progress lives on disk.

## Graph

Who runs next, what can run in parallel, where a human interrupts, how you recover after a crash. LangGraph for cycles and HITL. Compile-once static DAGs when the map does not change.

## OWA

Orchestrator–Worker–Adversary. Judgment and generation stay apart.

## Swarm size

Two or three isolated specialists usually peak. Five to seven without isolation regress.
